INDEPENDENT GUIDEBEFORE SUBSCRIBING

Choose AI tools by the task—not by the headline

Image generation, research assistance, language learning, and messaging automation use different kinds of AI. An attractive feature list is not enough to know whether a product will help your business. Start with a real task you already perform and define the time, quality, or cost improvement you hope to see.

AI-generated material can contain factual errors, and model outputs may not be suitable for confidential or regulated work. Review every important result before relying on it.

Practical evaluation checklist

Test useful workflows

  • Try representative tasks rather than generic demos
  • Review accuracy and source support where relevant
  • Check supported languages and export formats
  • Understand human review and editing needs

Review product terms

  • Usage limits, credits, and subscription renewals
  • Privacy, retention, and data-use settings
  • Commercial-use rights for generated content
  • Integration and team account permissions

Do not submit passwords, sensitive customer records, or confidential business information to an AI tool without a clearly appropriate and authorized data-handling arrangement.

A question to consider

Should every business use several AI subscriptions?

No. Start with one real bottleneck and choose the service that handles it reliably. More tools do not automatically improve results.
