How to choose between a store builder and a website builder

An ecommerce store, a service-business website, and a technical WordPress project have different requirements. A hosted builder can reduce initial setup work, while a more flexible website platform may require more maintenance and technical decisions.

Start by identifying what visitors need to do: book a service, browse information, purchase products, or contact your team. Then compare platforms that support those tasks.

Selection checklist

Content and experience

  • Support for your pages, language, and navigation
  • Mobile-friendly designs and accessible forms
  • Search-friendly page URLs and metadata controls
  • Domain connection and content portability

Commerce and operating cost

  • Products, inventory, checkout, and taxes if needed
  • App or plugin fees beyond the main subscription
  • Renewal costs and payment provider compatibility
  • Support, backups, and export or migration options

A free or low introductory price may include platform branding, product limits, or future upgrades. Check the full cost and the export policy before building a large website.

Common question

Should a small business choose Shopify or a general site builder?

Shopify focuses on ecommerce. A general site builder may be enough for a service website without product checkout. Compare the tasks and costs of your project.
