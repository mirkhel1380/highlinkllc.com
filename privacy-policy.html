01 / OVERVIEW

Who and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy relates to the Highlink website, its publicly accessible content, and communications sent to the contact address listed below. Highlink provides information about business registration, websites and hosting, AI and SEO, and online security resources.

It does not govern the websites, apps, registration portals, purchase flows, or account systems of independent providers that may be linked from Highlink. Those services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

02 / INFORMATION YOU SHARE

Messages and other information you provide

If you contact Highlink by email or through the contact form, we may receive your name, email address, selected inquiry topic, subject line, message, and any other details you choose to provide. We use this information to understand the inquiry, respond where appropriate, and manage relevant correspondence.

Contact form delivery: The form on our Contact page submits information to FormSubmit, an independent form-delivery service, which processes the fields you submit and forwards the message to our contact email address. The service may also process technical details needed to deliver submissions and prevent spam. You can choose to send us a direct email instead of using the form. Please review the form service’s own privacy practices when deciding whether to submit information.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, identity documents, sensitive personal information, or other confidential records unless a specific secure process has been agreed upon. The general contact email is intended for ordinary inquiries, not sensitive submissions.

We do not require visitors to supply personal information simply to read the public guides and service pages.

03 / WEBSITE OPERATIONS

Technical and usage information

As with most websites, the infrastructure used to deliver pages may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times, browser or device characteristics, referring URLs, and request or error details. Hosting and security providers may use this information to deliver the site, detect misuse, troubleshoot problems, or maintain reliability.

Where website analytics or performance measurement is enabled, relevant services may also process information about page visits and interactions. The availability and operation of such tools can change as the website is maintained. Highlink should not be understood to collect every technical data category in every visit.

04 / SITE PREFERENCES

Light and dark theme preference

Highlink offers light and dark appearance settings. The theme control stores a simple preference in your browser's local storage under the name hl-theme. This allows the website to remember your chosen appearance as you move between pages or return using the same browser.

This preference is stored on your device rather than being an account profile. You can change the setting with the theme button or clear it through your browser's site-data settings. The appearance preference is not, by itself, a personal identification record.

05 / COOKIES & SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies, browser storage, and embedded resources

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites or third-party services can store in a browser. Similar technologies include local storage and technologies used to measure visits or referrals. The Highlink theme preference uses local storage as described above.

Third-party services used to deliver website resources, images, analytics, or referral links may process technical data or use cookies where their technology and your browser settings allow. For example, some logos or icons may be requested from an outside image service; your browser connects to that service to load the resource.

You can manage cookies, site storage, and tracking protections using your browser settings. Blocking particular technologies may affect the way some external services work. Any legally required consent mechanism must be implemented when optional tracking technologies are deployed in jurisdictions that require it.

06 / AFFILIATE & REFERRAL LINKS

What happens when you select an external offer

Some Highlink links are affiliate or referral links. Clicking one may first pass through a third-party referral network before reaching the provider's website. Those services may process referral identifiers, click information, device or browser data, and other information as described in their own policies to attribute a visit or qualifying purchase.

Highlink may receive a commission or referral payment if an eligible action is completed. We do not control the cookies, attribution rules, account information, or privacy practices applied by the third-party network or final provider. Review those services' privacy notices before you submit information or make a purchase.

Important distinction: Browsing an article on Highlink and opening a third-party provider link are different activities. The provider's own privacy rules apply once you interact with its systems.
07 / PURPOSES

Why information may be processed

  • Communications: to respond to emails and manage inquiries or feedback.
  • Website operation: to deliver pages and maintain site functionality.
  • Security: to investigate abuse, unexpected errors, or technical issues.
  • Preferences: to remember a selected light or dark appearance.
  • Measurement: to understand performance or referrals when relevant services are enabled.
  • Legal responsibilities: to retain or disclose information where applicable law requires it.

Where applicable privacy law requires a particular legal basis, processing must be supported by the appropriate basis, such as providing a requested response, legitimate operational interests, legal requirements, or consent for technologies that need it.

08 / OTHER SERVICES

Service providers and information sharing

Information relevant to operating Highlink may be handled by service providers involved in hosting, network delivery, security, or email. Their access and processing depend on the services actually configured.

When you choose a third-party link, the relevant network and provider may receive information from your browser as part of that visit. If you sign up, order, pay, or submit a form on the provider's website, the information you give them is handled under their own terms and privacy notice.

Information may also need to be disclosed in response to a valid legal obligation or to protect rights and website security, to the extent permitted or required by applicable law.

09 / SAFEGUARDS

Retention and security

Different kinds of information can be retained for different periods. For example, correspondence may be kept as needed to answer an inquiry, document a relevant business communication, resolve an issue, or satisfy applicable requirements. Hosting and other providers apply their own technical retention settings.

Highlink aims to handle information responsibly and limit access where practical. However, no online communication, service, or storage method is completely risk-free. Do not send highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

10 / YOUR CHOICES

Controls and privacy-related requests

You can choose not to send a contact email, clear local website storage, adjust cookie settings, or avoid visiting third-party links. Where applicable law gives you rights relating to personal information held by Highlink, you may request access, correction, deletion, or other available action.

Contact us at the address below and describe the information or communication involved. Depending on the request and applicable law, we may need to verify that it relates to you, and some information may need to be retained where permitted or required by law.

For information collected directly by an external provider, contact that provider using its privacy information. Highlink generally cannot manage a separate provider account or erase data stored in that provider's systems.

11 / GLOBAL ACCESS

Visitors from different countries

Highlink is accessible online, and visitors or service providers may be located in different countries. Depending on the website infrastructure and the company operating an external service, information may be processed in locations outside your country of residence.

Privacy rights and obligations vary by jurisdiction. If local law requires additional notices, safeguards, choices, or consent, those requirements should be addressed in the services and technologies used for affected visitors.

12 / AGE & AUDIENCE

Children's privacy

Highlink's content is primarily about business and professional services and is not directed toward collecting personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to our contact address, they may contact us to ask about an appropriate response.

13 / CONTACT & UPDATES

Policy changes and contact information

We may update this Privacy Policy when site features, technologies, service relationships, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be made available on this page with a revised date. Please review it from time to time if you use Highlink regularly.

For privacy questions or requests relating to information Highlink may hold, please email us. For issues involving a specific provider, it may also be necessary to contact that provider directly.
