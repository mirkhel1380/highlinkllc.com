What matters when comparing UK formation services

A company formation package is useful only if it provides the documentation, address arrangements, and support your proposed company actually needs. The cheapest first-year package may exclude registered office services or include renewals you have not planned for.

Use a shortlist of realistic requirements before looking at promotional pricing. Incorporation is one step in managing a compliant company, not the end of your responsibilities.

Selection checklist

Formation essentials

  • Eligibility and name availability
  • Documents and information needed for the filing
  • Government fees versus provider charges
  • How registration status and documents are delivered

Post-formation services

  • Registered office address and renewals
  • Mail forwarding or scanning limits
  • Help with corrections or company changes
  • Whether accounting and tax services are optional

Keep company details accurate and check the current government requirements, including identity verification and ongoing filing obligations. A provider package is not a substitute for professional tax or legal advice.

Common question

Are a registered office and filing assistance included in all packages?

Not necessarily. Check each provider's exact plan contents, additional charges, and renewal terms before ordering.
