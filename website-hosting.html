How to Choose the Right Solution

For beginners: A website builder may offer a simpler way to create a website without managing technical infrastructure.

For business websites: Compare hosting reliability, customer support, security features, storage, and renewal prices.

For growing projects: VPS or cloud hosting may provide additional control and flexibility, depending on your technical needs.

Always review the provider's current pricing, renewal terms, and service limitations before making a purchase.
