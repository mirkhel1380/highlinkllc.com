BUYER'S GUIDEREVIEW BEFORE ORDERING

What to check before registering a domain name

A domain is part of your business identity, so the decision should last longer than a promotion. Check that customers can remember and spell the name, that the registration is under an account you control, and that you understand the renewal price.

Domains also connect to business email, DNS, and security settings. Switching providers later may be possible, but some transfers are subject to timing rules and account verification.

Selection checklist

Price and ownership

  • First-year cost compared with renewal fees
  • Registration term and automatic renewal settings
  • Registrant and billing account details
  • Domain transfer and expiration policies

Day-to-day management

  • DNS records and domain connection tools
  • Security such as account two-factor authentication
  • Available privacy protections where eligible
  • Support for email, redirects, and subdomains

Some domain extensions have special eligibility rules or higher renewal pricing. Check the particular extension rather than assuming every domain has the same terms.

Common question

Can I use a domain with hosting bought elsewhere?

Often yes. Many registrars allow you to point your DNS records to a separate hosting provider, but the technical steps depend on the services.
