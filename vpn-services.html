How to compare VPN services for everyday privacy

A VPN encrypts traffic between your device and a VPN server. It can be useful on untrusted networks and for some privacy needs, but it does not make a device anonymous or automatically stop phishing, malware, or account theft.

Choose a service based on what you actually need: supported devices, connection reliability, privacy information, ease of use, and ongoing cost. Promotional speed or privacy claims should be evaluated carefully.

Key things to check

Practical comparison

  • Compatibility with the devices you own
  • Clear terms about subscription renewal and refunds
  • Transparent explanations of privacy and data handling
  • Reliable support and straightforward account controls

Security habits to keep

  • Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • Update software and browsers promptly
  • Verify links before sharing personal information
  • Protect devices with suitable security tools

Do not confuse using a VPN with having complete privacy. Websites, apps, logged-in accounts, and unsafe downloads can still expose information.

Frequently asked question

Does a VPN replace antivirus or account security?

No. A VPN protects certain connections, whereas other risks still need account protections, software updates, safe browsing, and appropriate device security.
