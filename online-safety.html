Online Safety Guide

Practical tips and resources to help protect your accounts, devices, personal information, and everyday online activity.

Build Safer Online Habits

Good online security combines reliable tools with careful everyday habits. These basic practices can help reduce common digital security risks.

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts and avoid reusing the same password across multiple services.

Two-Step Verification

Enable two-factor or multi-factor authentication when available to add another layer of account security.

Safer Browsing

Check website addresses carefully and be cautious with unexpected links, downloads, messages, and attachments.

Software Updates

Keep operating systems, browsers, apps, and security software updated with supported security updates.

Account Privacy

Review privacy and security settings on important accounts and limit unnecessary sharing of personal information.

Device Security

Protect devices with appropriate security settings, trusted software, and secure access methods.
