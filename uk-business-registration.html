Understand the UK LTD route before choosing a provider

UK company formation requires appropriate company details, a registered office address, and ongoing compliance after incorporation. Third-party providers can organize a filing package or add business address services, but founders should know what is included and what remains their responsibility.

What you should prepare

Registration decisions

  • Confirm the proposed company name and key details
  • Understand director and identity verification requirements
  • Arrange a suitable registered office address
  • Review government fees and provider charges

Keeping the company up to date

  • Maintain official records and contact details
  • Plan accounts and confirmation requirements
  • Understand tax registration and reporting separately
  • Check renewal terms for address or support services

Rules can change, and an incorporated company is not the same thing as a guaranteed bank account, visa, or regulated business authorization. Verify current requirements through official sources and qualified advisers.

Questions and next steps

Can someone outside the UK register a limited company?

UK companies may be formed by eligible overseas founders, but requirements and circumstances vary. Review identity, registered office, and ongoing compliance rules before filing.
