Plan your U.S. business registration carefully

Forming a U.S. LLC involves more than selecting a service package. The choice of state, entity name, registered agent, operating arrangements, and ongoing filings may affect your cost and responsibilities. A provider can assist with part of the registration process, but you should still understand the paperwork being submitted.

What you should prepare

Before filing

  • Identify the state where your company will be formed
  • Check name availability through official state resources
  • Understand registered agent eligibility in that state
  • Estimate required state fees and future reporting

After formation

  • Keep official formation records safely
  • Understand any tax registrations or licenses you may need
  • Maintain accurate contact and company information
  • Plan annual reports or recurring state requirements

A company formed in one state may still have obligations elsewhere when it conducts business there. Review your real activities and applicable state rules rather than choosing solely on a cheap registration fee.

Questions and next steps

Do I need to purchase a full formation package?

Not always. Some founders file directly with their state, while others prefer provider assistance. Compare time, eligibility, fees, and required support.
