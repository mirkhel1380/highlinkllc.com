What to compare before paying for LLC formation

An online formation provider can simplify document preparation and filing, but the service does not eliminate state filing fees or the responsibilities that follow registration. A low advertised starting price should be evaluated alongside registered agent costs, optional extras, and the state's ongoing requirements.

The right choice depends on where you form the LLC, whether you need a registered agent, who will maintain records, and whether you can complete the filing yourself.

Questions worth checking before purchase

Compare the full cost

  • Required state formation filing fee
  • Any provider service fee and optional add-ons
  • Registered agent cost, including later renewals
  • Annual reports and other ongoing state obligations

Check the process

  • Who prepares and submits the state paperwork
  • How corrections or rejected filings are handled
  • When state confirmation is expected—not guaranteed
  • Where official documents will be delivered

A company can be registered without automatically receiving every license, tax registration, or bank account it needs. Check your state and business activity separately before buying a package.

Frequently asked question

Does a $0 service fee mean the LLC is completely free?

No. State filing fees normally still apply, and optional features or ongoing compliance services may carry separate charges.
