SECURITY DECISION GUIDEPRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Choose device protection without relying on a single feature

Security software can contribute to protecting an eligible computer or device, but it is only one part of a safer setup. Check compatibility, maintenance expectations, support, and the latest capabilities directly with the provider.

Even good protection tools do not remove the need for careful account habits, regular software updates, and backups. Avoid assuming any tool can prevent every threat.

Key things to check

Evaluate your devices

  • Supported operating systems and device types
  • Whether protection overlaps with existing tools
  • Performance impact and automatic update options
  • How alerts and support requests are handled

Understand the subscription

  • Number of covered devices or users
  • Renewal price and cancellation terms
  • Available support and account recovery options
  • Any add-on services or separate charges

Security products should only be installed on devices you own or are authorized to manage, with suitable consent and organizational policies.

Frequently asked question

Can security software guarantee my accounts are safe?

No. Software helps with some threats, but safe account access, multifactor authentication, updates, and backup plans remain important.
