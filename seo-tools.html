Moz Pro vs Moz Local: which one do you actually need?

The names are similar, but the products solve different problems. Moz Pro focuses on researching organic search opportunities and monitoring a website's SEO performance. Moz Local focuses on maintaining consistent business information across local listings, monitoring reviews, and supporting location-based visibility.

Choosing between them starts with a simple question: Are you trying to improve a website's organic search performance, or manage how a physical business location appears to nearby customers? Your answer can save you from subscribing to the wrong tool.

OPTION 01

When Moz Pro is a better fit

Moz Pro may be useful if you publish content, run an online store, manage a company website, or provide SEO services. Instead of guessing which pages need attention, you can use keyword research, website crawling, rank tracking, and competitive analysis to organize your work.

Good reasons to consider it

  • You want to discover keywords relevant to your business.
  • You need to monitor selected organic search rankings.
  • You want to identify crawl or technical SEO issues.
  • You compare competitors' search visibility.

Reasons to wait

  • You have not yet established a useful website or content strategy.
  • You only need a few free search performance reports.
  • Your main challenge is maintaining local business listings.
  • The current plan limits exceed your budget or needs.

OPTION 02

When Moz Local makes more sense

Local discovery is different from general website SEO. For an eligible location-based business such as a clinic, store, or local service provider, accurate business details and review activity can matter as much as the website itself. Moz Local is designed to help manage those location signals from one platform.

Good reasons to consider it

  • You need consistent business names, addresses, and contact information.
  • You manage customer reviews or reputation across locations.
  • You want visibility into local rankings and listing activity.
  • You manage more than one eligible business location.

Reasons to wait

  • Your business has no qualifying local presence.
  • Your country or location is not currently supported.
  • You are mainly seeking general keyword research or site audits.
  • Your listing details are inaccurate and need to be corrected first.

Eligibility matters: Moz's current documentation describes Moz Local availability for businesses in the U.S., Canada, and the UK. Check the latest supported locations and plan details before ordering.

A practical checklist before paying for either product

  1. Define one measurable goal. For Moz Pro, identify target search terms or site improvements; for Moz Local, identify listing accuracy or local customer discovery goals.
  2. Check the basics first. Confirm that you have access to the website or business profiles you intend to manage.
  3. Check eligibility and plan limits. Compare supported regions, number of locations or campaigns, tracked keywords, and other relevant limits.
  4. Compare the total cost. Review monthly or annual billing, renewals, and add-on costs on the provider's official checkout pages.
  5. Use the data consistently. Software provides research and measurement; actual improvements still require changes to your pages, listings, or content.

Common questions about Moz Pro and Moz Local

Is Moz Local included in Moz Pro?

No. Moz's documentation describes Moz Local and Moz Pro as separate products that require separate subscriptions.

Can an online-only business use Moz Local?

Moz Local focuses on eligible location-based businesses. Online-only businesses without a qualifying location may be better served by broader SEO tools such as Moz Pro. Always check the provider's current eligibility rules.

Can I use both Moz products?

Yes, if your business needs both website SEO research and local listing management. Evaluate whether the benefit of two separate subscriptions fits your budget.

Will either tool guarantee higher Google rankings?

No. SEO software can provide insight and help prioritize work, but search results depend on many factors beyond a subscription.

Where can I check exact prices?

Use the provider's current plan and checkout pages. Pricing, available features, promotions, and renewal terms may change.
