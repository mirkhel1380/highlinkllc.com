BEFORE YOU CHOOSEBUYER CHECKLIST

How to choose a U.S. registered agent

A registered agent is the person or business designated to receive official legal and government notices for an entity. Most U.S. states require an LLC to maintain an eligible agent with an address in the formation state. Requirements and eligibility vary by jurisdiction.

A registered agent service should be judged on reliability and how notices reach you—not only on its first-year promotional price.

Questions worth checking before purchase

Verify practical requirements

  • Whether the agent has an eligible in-state street address
  • When notices are received and forwarded to you
  • How the service handles changes to your contact details
  • How you can replace or cancel the service

Compare ongoing terms

  • First-year price versus annual renewal price
  • Document scanning, forwarding, and notification options
  • Fees for multiple states if you expand later
  • Whether compliance reminders are included or charged separately

A registered agent address is not automatically a business mailing address, operating location, or substitute for every legal address your business may need. Check each purpose separately.

Frequently asked question

Can I appoint myself as an LLC registered agent?

In some states you may qualify if you meet the legal address and availability requirements. Check the state rules before choosing between self-service and a commercial agent.
