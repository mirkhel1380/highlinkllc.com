Good automation

Keep flows short and clear

Automation is most useful when it answers a specific need. Avoid sending unnecessary messages, make opt-outs clear, and review platform rules for customer communications.

  • Set one goal per flow
  • Keep messages understandable
  • Offer human support where appropriate
INDEPENDENT GUIDEBEFORE SUBSCRIBING

Design a useful customer messaging workflow

Chatbot software is most effective when the conversation has a clear purpose: helping a customer find information, collecting a permitted inquiry, or sending an approved update. Automating every message can frustrate visitors if the flow is difficult to exit.

Before paying for a chatbot platform, consider which messaging channels your audience uses and which platform rules govern automated responses.

Practical evaluation checklist

Create a responsible flow

  • Start with one useful customer question
  • Offer a clear route to a person when needed
  • Keep answers accurate and easy to update
  • Make opt-outs and contact preferences understandable

Compare integrations and limits

  • Supported channels and message categories
  • Contact, workflow, and subscription limits
  • Consent and privacy requirements
  • Connecting to existing customer tools

Only send messages to people who have given the necessary permissions under the platform rules and applicable law. A customer-facing chatbot must not mislead people about whether they are speaking with a person.

A question to consider

Can a chatbot replace a support team completely?

Not reliably. Simple tasks can be automated, but unusual customer problems, disputes, and sensitive situations often need human review.
