Understand UK registered office address services

A UK limited company must keep an appropriate registered office address where official correspondence can be received. A service address may help eligible businesses meet certain requirements, but a registered office is not automatically a trading location or a replacement for every other address.

Consider whether the provider offers the right type of address and how government and business mail is delivered to you.

Questions worth checking before purchase

Check address suitability

  • Eligibility of the location for your company
  • Which types of official mail are accepted
  • Mail scanning, forwarding, and delivery methods
  • What happens if your subscription ends

Check the total package

  • First-year cost versus renewal price
  • Whether director service addresses cost extra
  • Restrictions on using the address for other purposes
  • How quickly address changes can be processed

Never describe an address as your physical trading location unless that is accurate. Check current Companies House rules and the service's own terms for your situation.

Frequently asked question

Is a registered office the same as a trading address?

No. The registered office is the company's official correspondence address. A trading address refers to where business activity actually takes place.
